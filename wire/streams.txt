# THE WIRE, STREAMS: a call opened as a stream is answered at once; its chunks follow under CREDIT, then its end
# or its condition. A stream the host feeds into the call goes the other way under the far side's credit
# (wire/README.md). Format as in calls.txt; the lines of a stream carry no id.
#
